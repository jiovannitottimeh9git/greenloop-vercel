RECYCLING CATEGORIES

What can you recycle?

Use this guide to sort common household and small-business waste. Always follow the instructions of the collection centre or recycler near you.

Plastics

Accept: PET water bottles, clear or blue bottles, pure water sachets, hard plastic containers, crates and beverage cans.

Prepare: Empty, rinse and dry. Remove labels and caps where requested. Keep plastic types separate if the recycler asks.

Becomes pellets, sachets, roofing sheets, bags, benches and new containers.

Paper & cardboard

Accept: Clean newspapers, office paper, cartons, flattened cardboard and paper bags.

Prepare: Keep dry, remove food residue, flatten boxes and separate glossy coated paper from plain paper where possible.

Becomes recycled paper, cartons, packaging and compost-friendly fibre.

Glass

Accept: Glass bottles and jars, especially returnable drink bottles.

Prepare: Rinse, remove metal lids and keep broken glass separate and safely wrapped. Do not mix glass with ceramics.

Becomes new bottles, jars or construction aggregate.

Metals

Accept: Aluminium drink cans, steel food tins, scrap metal and clean foil.

Prepare: Empty and rinse. Flatten cans where possible and keep ferrous and non-ferrous metals separate if the recycler requires it.

Becomes new cans, metal products and recycled scrap.

Organic & garden waste

Accept: Fruit and vegetable peels, food scraps, leaves, grass and small garden trimmings.

Prepare: Keep food waste separate from plastics and glass. Avoid meat and oily waste if your composting service does not accept them.

Becomes compost and fertiliser for farms and gardens.

E-waste & batteries

Accept: Phones, chargers, computers, small appliances, cables and batteries through approved e-waste collectors.

Prepare: Remove batteries where possible, tape battery terminals, wipe personal data from devices and never burn wires or electronics.

Recovered metals and parts return to manufacturing; harmful materials are handled safely.

RECYCLING TIPS

Recycle right in five steps.

Good habits at home make every collection and drop-off more valuable.

01

Sort at source

Keep plastics, paper, glass, metal and organic waste in separate bags or containers from the moment you discard them.

02

Empty, rinse and dry

Food and liquid contamination can make otherwise recyclable materials unusable. A quick rinse makes a big difference.

03

Keep paper dry

Wet cardboard and paper are difficult to recover. Store them under cover until collection or drop-off.

04

Flatten and compact

Flatten boxes and crush bottles where allowed. This saves space in bags, bins and collection vehicles.

05

Check before you drop

Call the centre or ask the collector what they accept. Never place batteries, electronics or hazardous waste in ordinary recycling bags.

Do not burn or dump waste.

Burning plastics and e-waste releases harmful fumes. If there is no drop-off point near you, request a GreenLoop pickup or ask your local assembly about the nearest collection point.
